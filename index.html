The Modern Recycling Standard

We eliminate the friction and distrust of traditional street scrap transactions with certified hardware and real-time software.

Traditional Street Kabadiwala

Friction-Filled & Unreliable

  • Rigged Manual Scales: Spring scales manipulated to underweigh scrap by 20% to 35%.
  • Unpredictable Schedules: Shouting collectors with irregular, random street visits.
  • Landfill Leakage: Non-valuable domestic waste discarded improperly into city landfills.
Result: 80% of urban recyclables lost into landfills annually.
The Kabadiwallah Standard

Transparent, Dignified & Digital

  • Certified Digital Scales: Bluetooth-paired, verifiable scales showing exact kilograms.
  • Guaranteed Doorstep Slots: Pre-scheduled pickup windows confirmed via WhatsApp & SMS.
  • Direct Circular Off-takers: 100% sorted scrap channeled directly to verified recycling plants.
Result: Fair payouts & clean zero-landfill diversion. 100% Traceable
